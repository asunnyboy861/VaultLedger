| Psychological Pricing | $4.99 < $5.00 = impulse purchase zone |
| Market Validation | Simple Offline Budget free but minimal features = $4.99 full-featured is reasonable upgrade |
| User Profile | Privacy-conscious users typically have payment willingness, $4.99 is low barrier |
| App Store Convention | Finance category paid apps median $3.99-$9.99 |
