VaultLedger Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2025

TL;DR: VaultLedger stores all your financial data locally on your device. We never collect, transmit, or share your personal or financial data with any third party.

1. Data Collection

VaultLedger is an offline-first finance management application. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data on external servers. All data you enter remains exclusively on your device.

  • Financial Data: Transaction records, account balances, budget information, and net worth snapshots are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework with optional AES-256 encryption.
  • Imported Data: Data imported from CSV or PDF bank statements is processed entirely on-device and never leaves your device.
  • No Analytics: We do not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs that collect user data.

2. Data Storage & Security

Your data is secured using industry-standard encryption:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) for app access
  • All data stored in the app's sandboxed container on your device
  • Backup files are encrypted and can only be restored within the app

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. Period.

  • No data is sent to our servers (we don't have servers)
  • No data is shared with third-party analytics or advertising companies
  • No data is sold or transferred to any third party
  • Export features (CSV, backup) are initiated only by you and saved to locations you choose

4. Biometric Data

VaultLedger uses Face ID / Touch ID for app authentication only. Biometric data is never accessed, stored, or transmitted by our app. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS through the LocalAuthentication framework.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Deleting the app removes all data permanently
  • You can delete individual transactions, accounts, or budgets at any time
  • Backup files stored in your chosen locations must be manually deleted

6. Children's Privacy

VaultLedger does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is intended for general audience use for personal finance management.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Your Rights

As all data is stored locally, you have complete control over your information. You can:

  • View all stored data within the app
  • Export your data at any time in CSV format
  • Create encrypted backups of your data
  • Delete any or all data at any time
